Dr. Jimeng Sun
Health Innovation Professor
University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign
Dr. Jimeng Sun is a Health Innovation Professor at the Siebel School of Computing and Data Science and Carle Illinois College of Medicine at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign, where he leads groundbreaking research at the intersection of artificial intelligence and healthcare. As co-founder of Keiji AI, he is pioneering the transformation of clinical trials through advanced AI optimization and predictive modeling, accelerating drug development and improving trial success rates.
Research Excellence & Impact
Dr. Sun's research portfolio spans the full spectrum of AI-driven healthcare innovation, from clinical AI systems and drug discovery to AI for clinical trials and machine learning for biosignals. His work has produced transformative advances in:
Clinical AI Systems & Healthcare Analytics: Developing interpretable models like RETAIN for healthcare prediction, building sophisticated patient similarity measures, temporal clinical event prediction models, and designing AI systems for medication recommendation and clinical outcome prediction that are actively deployed in healthcare settings
Drug Discovery & Development: Creating novel molecular optimization frameworks, drug-target interaction prediction models, and AI-driven therapeutic discovery platforms including the acclaimed Therapeutics Data Commons
AI for Clinical Trials: Pioneering patient-trial matching systems, clinical trial outcome prediction models, and optimization frameworks for trial design and patient recruitment through advanced deep learning and graph neural networks
Machine Learning for Biosignals: Advancing expert-level sleep staging with deep neural networks, creating automated seizure classification systems, and developing AI models for EEG interpretation and cardiac monitoring from physiological data streams
With over 40,000 citations and an h-index of 101, Dr. Sun has authored more than 500 publications in top-tier venues including Nature, NEJM AI, and leading AI conferences. His research has been recognized with prestigious awards and has directly influenced clinical practice through collaborations with major healthcare institutions.
Industry Collaboration
Named one of the Top 100 AI Leaders in Drug Discovery and Advanced Healthcare, Dr. Sun bridges academic research with real-world healthcare applications. Through Keiji AI and partnerships with industry leaders including IQVIA, Medidata, and GE Healthcare, his innovations are transforming how clinical trials are designed, conducted, and analyzed.
His collaborative network spans premier healthcare institutions including Massachusetts General Hospital, Beth Israel Deaconess, Northwestern, Vanderbilt, and OSF Healthcare, ensuring his research addresses the most pressing challenges in modern medicine.
Academic Background
Dr. Sun received his B.S. and M.Phil. in Computer Science from the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology and his Ph.D. from Carnegie Mellon University.
