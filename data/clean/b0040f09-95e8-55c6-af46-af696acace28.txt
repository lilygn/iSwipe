Active Research Themes
Low Latency Satellite Connectivity
We aim to enable near-realtime connectivity across Low Earth Orbit Satellites in space and devices on Earth through networking and AI innovations.
Recent Publications: MobiCom 2024 (Best Community Paper award), HotNets 2024, NSDI 2024, Mobicom 2023, SIGCOMM 2021, HotNets 2020
Machine Learning for Next Generation Networks
Modern wireless networks are complex systems with varied capabilities and user needs. We are building robust and verifiable ML models to make optimum use of their capabilities.
Recent Publications: ICLR 2025, NSDI 2023, MobiCom 2021, MobiCom 2020
Localization and Sensing
Our work focuses on enabling accurate location and sensing of devices, people, and robots using radio signals for applications in smart homes, healthcare, indoor navigation, and augmented reality.
Recent Publications: MobiSys 2024, Mobicom 2023, NSDI 2022, Mobicom 2020.
Rural Connectivity and Data-driven Agriculture
We are studying the challenges and solutions for enabling high-fidelity connectivity for rural applications like digital agriculture and e-learning.
Recent Publications: NSDI 2024, NSDI 2022, COMPASS 2019 (Best Paper award), NSDI 2017 (product at Microsoft)
