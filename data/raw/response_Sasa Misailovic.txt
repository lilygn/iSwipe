                <h1>Sasa Misailovic</h1> Pronunciation: /<i>Sa-sha Mee-sail-oveech</i>/

                <p style="margin-bottom:1em"> </p>


	       <p style="margin-bottom:1em">I am an Associate Professor in the <a href="http://cs.illinois.edu/" target="_blank">Siebel School of Computing and Data Science</a>
			at the <a href="http://illinois.edu/" target="_blank">University of
			Illinois at Urbana-Champaign</a>. I&nbsp;received my PhD from <a href="http://eecs.mit.edu/" target="_blank">MIT</a> in Summer 2015. 

	       <p style="margin-bottom:1em"> My research interests include programming
	       languages, compilers, and software engineering,
	       with an emphasis on improving performance, energy
	       efficiency, and resilience in the face of software
	       errors and approximation opportunities. <a href="research.html">Read More >></a> </p>
