<p><span class="normal"><b>Biosketch</b> </span></p>

<p><span class="normal">Sanjay J. Patel is a Professor of Electrical and Computer Engineering and Computer Science at the University of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign. He holds a PhD from University of Michigan, Ann Arbor in Computer Science and Engineering (1999). He is a world reknowned technologist, author, and entrepreneur who has been involved in multiple high-impact projects. Sanjay&rsquo;s research has focused on computer architecture, and massively parallel chip design, with a focus on emerging use cases. Sanjay has recieved the National Science Foundation CAREER Award, an IBM Faculty Partnership Award, and multiple best paper awards.</span></p>

<p><span class="normal">He is currently co-director of the IBM-Illinois Center for Cognitive Computing Systems Research, Senior Technology Advisor on the Rokwire Initiative, and Director of the Alchemy Foundry. For the past 15 years, he&rsquo;s been involved in numerous startup activities, including as CTO of Ageia Technologies (acquired by Nvidia) and as CEO and co-founder of Personify, Inc (acquired by Foxconn), and more recently, as advisor to ROKMETRO, Trova AI, DigitalGemini, and EarthSense.</span></p>

<p><span class="normal">He is co-author, with Prof. Yale N. Patt, on the widely-adopted textbook Introduction to Computing Systems: From Bits and Gates to C/C++ and Beyond.</span></p>
