I am a Teaching Assistant Professor at the Siebel School of Computing and Data Science at the University of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign. I am dedicated to teaching and making the learning process more effective and equitable. As an educator, I aim to help students construct the link between abstract concepts and practice while minimizing the cognitive load. I teach introductory programming, data management, and knowledge discovery.
