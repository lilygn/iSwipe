Lingming Zhang's main research interests lie in Software Engineering and Programming Languages, as well as their synergy with Machine Learning. To date, he has published over 100 research papers, with an h-index exceeding 50. His research has helped detect 1000+ bugs/vulnerabilities for open-source projects from Apache and GitHub, as well as software systems from eBay, Google, Meta/Facebook, Microsoft, NVIDIA, OctoML, Oracle, and Yahoo!. Recently, his group has built a number of pioneering techniques on LLM-based software testing, repair, and synthesis (including TitanFuzz, AlphaRepair, ChatRepair, and Agentless), with wide adoption in industry. His group has also released a series of competitive open-source code LLMs (including StarCoder2 and Magicoder), with over 1M downloads worldwide.
