About Me

I am a cryptographer and an assistant professor at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign. My research focuses on Secure Multiparty Computation (MPC), Zero Knowledge proofs, and Oblivious Data Structures. I am broadly interested in algorithmic techniques for reducing the cost of powerful cryptographic methods.

To learn more about my research, you can peruse my work below or have a look at my CV.
