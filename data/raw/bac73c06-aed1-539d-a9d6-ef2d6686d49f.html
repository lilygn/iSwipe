Dakshita Khurana

​Associate Professor of Computer Science
University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign
​Office: 4308 Siebel Center
I work on the theoretical foundations of cryptography and its interactions with quantum information. I am broadly interested in theoretical computer science.

You will also find an overview of my research on this page, and my CV here. 

I am lucky to be able to work with a wonderful set of students and postdocs! Please send me an email if you are interested in working with me.
