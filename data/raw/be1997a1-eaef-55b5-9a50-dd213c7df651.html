what we do

Autonomous systems and robots are becoming prevalent in our everyday lives and are changing the foundations of our way of life. However, the desirable impacts of autonomy are only achievable if the underlying algorithms can handle the unique challenges humans present: People tend to defy expected behaviors and do not conform to many of the standard assumptions made in robotics. To design safe, trustworthy systems, we must transform how intelligent robots interact, influence, and predict human agents.
